FAQ

The important stuff

How do I enter?

Buy $LUCK on Pons and hold at least 20,000 tokens. You're automatically eligible — no registration or wallet connect required.

What if I sell?

If your balance drops below 20K before the draw, you're removed from that round's eligible snapshot.

How is the pot split?

Each draw pays 80% to one eligible holder and 20% to the dev wallet. Both transfers happen automatically in the same on-chain transaction.

When are draws?

Every 1,200 seconds — exactly 20 minutes. The keeper runs automatically and the site updates winner history within seconds.

How is the pot funded?

A 5% creator tax on every buy and sell is paid in ETH to the POTLUCK vault through the Pons fee escrow. One holder wins 80% of the balance at draw time.
